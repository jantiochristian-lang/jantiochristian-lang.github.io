What to send

Sponsors and governments: the asset or transaction, the financing gap, what has already been attempted, the current stage and the parties involved.

Founders: what the product does, the problem it solves, who will pay for it, current traction and the capital being raised. A compelling demonstration matters; evidence of demand matters more.

Investors and limited partners: the investment objective, proposed relationship, areas of alignment and relevant decision timeline.

Companies and strategic partners: the market opportunity, proposed commercial relationship, geographic scope and the specific role contemplated for Sterling.

Institutions: the decision, capability or operating problem you are trying to address — not simply the technology you have been offered.

Conferences and media: the date, audience, format, proposed topic and role requested.

What we look for

Sterling is interested in exceptional people who combine analytical rigor, sound judgment, comfort with ambiguity and a genuine interest in emerging markets.

Experience in project finance, development finance, investment banking, private equity, venture capital or complex emerging-market transactions is valued, although not required at every level.

For technology investing, we value people who can interrogate a market, a business model, a cap table and the underlying technical architecture — and distinguish a compelling demonstration from a defensible company.

Applications

Opportunities will be posted when available. Exceptional candidates may also submit a curriculum vitae and a concise note describing the transactions, technologies or markets that interest them, the contribution they believe they can make and why Sterling is the right platform.
